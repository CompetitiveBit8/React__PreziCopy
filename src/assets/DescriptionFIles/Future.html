Frequently asked questions

  • Can I create a Prezi presentation from my PowerPoint slides?

    Yes. Prezi makes it easy to transform your existing PowerPoint presentation into something dynamic. Simply upload your PowerPoint slide deck to Prezi Present, and our tools will help you convert it into an interactive Prezi presentation. You can also import files from Google Slides or Keynote by exporting them as PDFs first.

  • What is Prezi’s AI presentation maker?

    Prezi AI is an AI presentation maker that helps you create a complete, polished presentation from a prompt or uploaded file, like a PDF, PPT, or DOC. Choose from a range of designers, and Prezi AI instantly generates a fully designed, ready-to-present deck, tailored to your content and style. It's like having a personal designer on demand, so you can go from idea to presentation in seconds.

  • How does Prezi's AI presentation designer work?

    Prezi's AI presentation designer turns your ideas into polished presentations effortlessly. Start by entering a prompt or uploading an existing file like a PDF or Word document. Then, choose your AI designer. Think of it like picking a personal designer who understands your style and builds the presentation around it.

    From there, Prezi AI instantly generates a fully designed, ready-to-present deck, complete with layouts, AI-generated images, and a visual theme tailored to both your content and your chosen designer.

    Once your deck is ready, you can refine it conversationally through the chat: ask it to adjust the tone, rework a slide, change the color palette, or add a new section.

  • Can Prezi create a PowerPoint (PPT) with AI?

    Yes. If you want an AI PowerPoint presentation maker workflow, you can use Prezi AI to draft your presentation quickly and then export or share it in the format your team needs, like exporting to a PowerPoint.

  • Can I turn an existing document into a presentation with AI?

    Yes. Upload a file or add a prompt, choose your AI designer, and Prezi AI instantly generates a fully designed presentation based on your content. Once your deck is generated, you can use the chat to refine it: reorder sections, update messaging, or adjust the design by simply describing what you want. You stay in control throughout.

  •  Is Prezi a free AI presentation tool?

    Yes, Prezi is available as a free presentation software with AI-powered features included. If you want unlimited access to Prezi AI, you can try a free trial or upgrade to a paid subscription. No matter which subscription you choose, you will be able to present with Prezi's unique format that makes content more engaging and memorable.

  • How is Prezi different from other AI presentation generators?

    While most AI presentation tools generate a rough outline for you to fill in, Prezi AI skips that step entirely. Enter a prompt or upload a file, choose an AI designer that matches your aesthetic, and instantly get a fully designed, ready-to-present deck — custom layouts, colors, and images tailored to your content and style. It's like having a personal designer who builds the presentation for you. Then, instead of manually tweaking elements, you refine through chat: just tell Prezi AI what to change, and it updates in real time.

  • What types of presentations can I create with Prezi AI?

    With Prezi AI, you can create presentations for work, school, and personal projects, including pitch decks, reports, lessons, and slide presentations. If you are looking to make a presentation with AI for work or school, Prezi AI can help you draft faster and present more effectively.

  • Does Prezi AI help with slide structure and talking points?

    Yes. Prezi AI handles structure automatically. The moment you submit a prompt or upload a file, it generates a logically organized, fully designed deck. If you want to adjust the flow, reframe sections, or sharpen talking points, just use the chat to describe the change, and Prezi AI will update it for you.

  • Can I edit the AI-generated presentation?

    Absolutely. Once Prezi AI generates your deck, you can refine it through the chat by describing what you'd like to change, whether that's the tone, a specific slide, the visual style, or the overall structure. You can also make direct edits to text and visuals so the final result matches your brand, audience, and goals.

  • Is Prezi good for creating slide decks with AI for teams?

    Yes. Prezi works well for collaborative workflows where multiple people need to review, refine, and align on messaging. If you’re looking for an AI presentation software that keeps presentations consistent across your organization with shared collaboration features and team-friendly controls, Prezi for Teams is an ideal solution.

  • What should I include in a prompt for the best AI presentation results?

    For the best results, include your audience, goal, topic, tone, and any must-have points. Example: “Create a 10-slide deck for mid-market HR leaders about onboarding software, highlight three benefits, include a comparison slide, and a strong CTA.” For even more prompt examples, you can explore our AI prompt library to get inspired.

  • Is Prezi AI suitable for beginners who are new to presentation design?

    Yes. Prezi AI is built to help beginners get a high-quality, fully designed deck instantly. Simply enter a prompt, choose an AI designer that fits your style, and your presentation is ready in seconds. If you want to adjust anything, the chat makes it easy: just describe what you'd like to change, and Prezi AI handles the rest.

How does Prezi AI help me create presentations?

Prezi AI helps with every stage of presentation creation, including:

  1. Outline: Use Prezi AI to help you structure the outline for your presentation.
  2. Design: Prezi AI can suggest design elements, color palettes, and layouts.
  3. Content: Prezi AI can help with generating text and visuals based on your outline.
  4. Refine: Review and edit the AI-generated content to ensure it aligns with your goals.

What are the benefits of AI for presentations?

Writing clear and concise AI prompts is essential for streamlining your workflow and generating high-quality presentations using AI presentation makers. Here are some key tips to consider when creating your presentation with Prezi AI:

  1. Define your focus: Be specific about the central topic of your presentation.
  2. Tailor for impact: Consider your audience and adjust the prompt accordingly.
  3. Paint a picture with words: Describe your desired visual style and tone.
  4. Keywords & tone: Include relevant keywords and specify the desired tone.
  5. Cost savings: By automating various aspects of presentation creation and delivery, the AI presentation maker can help you reduce costs associated with design and manual content adjustments.

By following these tips, you can effectively communicate your vision to Prezi AI and create impactful presentations that align with your goals. Remember to be specific and clear and keep your presentation description concise and easy-to-understand.

What is a generative AI presentation?

A generative AI presentation is one that is created or partially created using artificial intelligence. This can include generating ideas, designing slides and frames, or creating content.

What is the best way to deliver an AI presentation?

Whether you're delivering an AI presentation on business topics or educational concepts, make sure to tailor your content to your audience's knowledge and interests. Use clear language, break down complex concepts, and use visuals to improve understanding.

Also, make sure to engage your audience by asking questions, encouraging participation, and making the presentation interactive. Finally, practice your presentation speech and seek feedback. Furthermore, you can explore resources like Prezi templates or examples for inspiration.

Prezi AI helps in other ways, too, like audience engagement. Quickly tailor your content to your audience’s preferences and keep them interested for your entire presentation.

Also, you can bring your bullet points to life by turning them into visual stories with the help of AI. In a few clicks, you can turn dull bullet points into dynamic, animated slides.
